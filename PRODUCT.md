# PRODUCT.md

## Register
product

## Product
AUDSPLIT — Windows tray tool that routes each app’s audio to a chosen output device.

## Users & purpose
People at a desk with multiple speakers/headphones (Bluetooth + wired). Primary task: open tray → see apps making sound → pick a device per app.

## Personality
Sharp, neon-tech, direct. Tagline: AUDIO. YOUR WAY.

## Surface
System tray flyout only. Explicit controls: per-app device dropdown, Refresh, Reset all, live status line. No settings app, no sidebar, no nested product menus.

## Anti-references
Settings dashboards, sidebar nav, profile managers, volume-mixer clones with hidden routing, cream/beige AI UI, purple-on-white SaaS kits.
